# Land a Job Kit: Tailor Your Resume with Claude

This kit turns Claude into your personal resume writer. You enter your work history once, then ask Claude to tailor your resume for any job in seconds. It is built to get your resume past the automated screening systems (ATS) that most companies use.

You run it in **Claude Chat** at claude.com, right in your browser. No app to install, no files to manage.

---

## What's inside

- `work-history.md` - fill this in with your real experience, then paste it into your project
- `resume-prompt.md` - the ready-made prompt you paste into a new chat
- `README.md` - you're reading it

---

## Quickstart (one-time setup, then seconds per job)

1. **Sign in** at https://claude.com.
2. **Make a Project.** In the sidebar, click Projects, then New project. Name it `Job Search`.
3. **Add your work history.** Fill in `work-history.md` with your real experience and real numbers, then paste it into the project's instructions. You only do this once.
4. **Tailor a resume.** Start a new chat inside the project. Paste the prompt from `resume-prompt.md`, paste the full job posting where it says to, and send.
5. **Review and use it.** Claude writes the tailored resume in the chat. Copy it out, read it over, send it.

Every job after this is just: open the project, new chat, paste the prompt plus the posting. Your work history is already there.

---

## Prefer an app to the browser? Use Claude Code

This kit also works inside Claude Code (Anthropic's free desktop/terminal app). If you go that route, you get built-in `/resume` and `/cover-letter` commands, so there's no prompt to paste.

1. Install Claude Code (free): https://claude.com/claude-code
2. Put this whole folder on your computer, keeping the hidden `.claude` folder (that's what powers the commands).
3. Open the folder in Claude Code, fill in `work-history.md`, then type `/resume` and paste a job posting. Use `/cover-letter` the same way.

Your tailored documents save to an `output/` folder automatically.

---

## The one rule

Everything Claude writes comes from **your real experience** in your work history. It will never invent jobs, numbers, or qualifications you don't have. If you didn't do it, it won't be on the resume. That keeps you honest and safe in interviews.

You are the final editor. Read every tailored resume before you send it.
