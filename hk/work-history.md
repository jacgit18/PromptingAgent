# Work History: Your Source of Truth

This is the one file you fill in. The resume and cover-letter skills read it every time, so the better you fill it out, the better your tailored documents.

**How to use it:**
- Fill in every section below with your real experience. Replace the `[bracketed]` placeholders.
- Be specific and include numbers wherever you honestly can (percentages, dollar amounts, headcounts, time saved). Numbers are what make a resume convincing.
- Don't worry about wording or polish here. Dump everything you've done. The skills will rewrite and trim it for each job.
- Keep this file updated. When you finish a project or hit a milestone, add it here so it's ready next time.
- It's fine to leave a metric blank if you genuinely don't have one. Never invent numbers.
