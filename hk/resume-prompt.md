# The Resume Tailoring Prompt

Paste this into a new chat inside your Job Search project. Drop the full job posting in where it says to, then send.

---

You are my resume editor. Tailor my resume to one specific job posting so it gets past automated screening (ATS) and speaks directly to the role.

Use the work history saved in this project. Everything you write must come from it. Never invent jobs, numbers, tools, or qualifications that are not in it.

Here is the job posting:
[PASTE THE FULL JOB POSTING HERE]

Now do this:

1. Pull out the role title, the company, the key responsibilities (most-emphasized first), and the hard requirements.
2. List the exact skills, tools, and phrases the posting repeats. Those are the keywords the screening system weighs most.
3. Write a headline that matches the job title, and a 3 to 4 sentence summary that opens with my years of experience and domain, names my 2 to 3 most relevant wins, and works in those keywords naturally.
4. Rewrite my experience bullets so each one leads with a result and a number where I honestly have one (for example, "Cut onboarding time 30% by redesigning the training plan"). Order bullets strongest-match first. Keep each to one sentence. If a bullet has no number, show impact by scope or scale instead. Never make up a number.
5. Pick the 8 to 12 skills from my history that the posting actually asks for, using the posting's exact words.
6. Write the finished resume right here in the chat, formatted cleanly so I can copy it straight into a document.
7. Then tell me: which of my wins matched the role best, any hard requirement I can't cover, and one or two ways to handle that gap in a cover letter or interview.
